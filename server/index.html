serendipity

Rediscover the things you saved.

We all save links for later: an article, a recipe, a film, a project we meant to try. Later rarely comes.

Each time you visit your page, serendipity shows you a few of your saved links at random, so the things you once found worth keeping can find their way back to you.

No email needed. You'll get your own page at a quiet, three-word address.
